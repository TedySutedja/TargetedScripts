Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our web design, custom analytics dashboards, zero-overhead hosting, and automation services.

How is Targeted Scripts different from cheap $199 website builders or rigid templates?

Cheap templates usually copy-paste text into static pages that are hard to update, charge monthly hosting fees, and are prone to broken links. At Targeted Scripts, we engineer custom, ultra-fast web tools, interactive executive dashboards, and background automations tailored to your exact operational workflow, backed by direct local support here in New Zealand.

Do I need to pay monthly web hosting fees for my website?

In most cases, no! We architect lightweight, high-performance websites using modern zero-overhead cloud hosting architecture. You get SSL encryption, lightning-fast loading speeds, and global reliability without the recurring $30–$80/month server hosting costs required by traditional platforms.

Do I need coding or technical skills to update my website or view my dashboards?

Not at all. Everything we build is designed for complete simplicity. Your executive dashboards feed automatically from raw Google Sheets or financial files, and we handle all underlying code maintenance so you can focus entirely on running your business.

Can you connect custom dashboards to my existing spreadsheets or Google Workspace?

Yes! We specialize in turning cluttered Excel spreadsheets and Google Sheets into visual, real-time dashboards (highlighting cash runway, net surplus, expense breakdowns, and operational metrics) using Google Apps Script and custom web frameworks.
