Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our web design, custom analytics dashboards, and automation services.

How is Targeted Scripts different from cheap $199 website builders or rigid templates?

Cheap templates usually copy-paste text into static pages that are hard to update and prone to broken links. At Targeted Scripts, we don't just build static pages—we engineer custom, ultra-fast web tools, interactive executive dashboards, and background automations tailored to your exact operational workflow, backed by direct local support here in New Zealand.

Do I need coding or technical skills to update my website or view my dashboards?

Not at all. Everything we build is designed for complete simplicity. Your executive dashboards feed automatically from raw Google Sheets or financial files, and we handle all underlying code maintenance so you can focus entirely on running your business.

What exactly is covered under the $250/month Essential Care Plan?

The Essential Care Plan ensures your digital tools operate flawlessly. It includes priority bug fixes, regular monitoring of your automation data pipelines, website updates, and 1 hour of minor feature adjustments each month—for less than the cost of two daily Flat Whites.

Can you connect custom dashboards to my existing spreadsheets or Google Workspace?

Yes! We specialize in turning cluttered Excel spreadsheets and Google Sheets into visual, real-time dashboards (highlighting cash runway, net surplus, expense breakdowns, and operational metrics) using Google Apps Script and custom web frameworks.
